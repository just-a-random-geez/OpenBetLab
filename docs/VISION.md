# Vision

## Why OpenBetLab exists

Betting odds are full of numbers that look simple but are easy to
misunderstand. What does "2.50" really mean as a probability? How much margin
is built into a price? Why are parlays so much less likely to win than they
feel? Most tools that answer these questions are closed, commercial, or
designed to encourage betting.

OpenBetLab aims to be the opposite: an **open, transparent, well-tested
research foundation** for anyone who wants to understand the mathematics of
odds and probability, whether they are students, researchers, journalists,
regulators or curious people.

## Principles

1. **Honesty over hype.** We never claim to predict outcomes or to "beat the
   bookmaker". Every assumption is written down.
2. **Correctness first.** Every calculation is tested, including edge cases.
3. **Simplicity.** Small, readable code with almost no dependencies, so that a
   beginner can read and check it.
4. **Reproducibility.** Anyone should be able to re-run any result.
5. **Safety.** No connections to bookmakers, no placing bets, no handling
   money, no personal data.

## What OpenBetLab is not

- Not a prediction engine or "tipster".
- Not a betting bot, and not connected to any bookmaker or exchange.
- Not financial or betting advice.

## Who it is for

- Learners who want to understand odds and probability.
- Researchers who need a small, trustworthy, auditable toolkit.
- Anyone studying how betting markets price uncertainty, including the
  harms of gambling.

## A note on gambling harm

Understanding the maths usually shows how hard it is to make money from
betting. If gambling is causing you or someone you know problems, please
reach out to a local support service.
