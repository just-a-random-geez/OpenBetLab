# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want OpenBetLab to be a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone,
whatever their age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that help:

- being kind, patient and respectful, especially towards beginners;
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback;
- focusing on what is best for the community and the quality of the research;
- being honest about uncertainty and the limits of our methods.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks;
- sexualised language or imagery;
- publishing other people's private information without their permission;
- promoting gambling to vulnerable people or minors, or presenting the
  project as a way to "guarantee" winnings;
- any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests,
discussions) and when someone is officially representing the project
elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the project maintainers privately, using
the contact method on the maintainers' GitHub profiles, or GitHub's
**Report content** option on the offending comment or issue. Please do not
report conduct problems in a public issue. All reports will
be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy
will be respected.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and
other contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct, and may
temporarily or permanently ban contributors whose behaviour is harmful.
